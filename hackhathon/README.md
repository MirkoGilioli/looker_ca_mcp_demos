# 🔐 TheLook Escape Room: The Case of the Bleeding eCommerce

Welcome to **TheLook Escape Room Hackathon**! 

You and your team have just been hired as the Lead AI Data Investigators for **TheLook eCommerce**, a fast-growing fashion retail platform. On paper, the business generates over **$10M in gross sales**, but the CEO is sounding the emergency alarms: **cash reserves are dwindling, customers are complaining, and millions of dollars are vanishing into thin air.**

Your mission is to use **Looker Conversational Analytics** to interrogate the data, unlock 5 mystery rooms, uncover the root causes of the business collapse, and report the escape codes to save the company!

---

## 🧭 How to Play the Escape Room

1. **Autonomous Investigation**: You will NOT write SQL from scratch. Instead, interact conversationally with your **Looker Conversational AI Agent**.
2. **Room Progression**: To escape each room, you must solve the mystery riddle, extract the required metric/code, and document your findings.
3. **5 Escape Chambers**:
   * 🚪 **Room 1: The Vault of Vanishing Revenue** *(Top-line Leakage & Status Discrepancies)*
   * 🚪 **Room 2: The Hall of Cursed Inventory** *(Category Returns & Fit Disasters)*
   * 🚪 **Room 3: The Bermuda Triangle of Lost Deliveries** *(Fulfillment & Distribution Center Bottlenecks)*
   * 🚪 **Room 4: The Phantom Traffic Trap** *(Marketing Attribution & Cancellation Rings)*
   * 🚪 **Room 5: The Final Executive Escape Plan** *(Assembling the Master Diagnostic Dashboard)*

## ⚡ Ground Rules & Setup

* **Model**: `thelook_ecommerce`
* **Explores**: `order_items`, `events`
* **Looker Mode**: Ensure you are connected to your Looker instance and have access to Conversation Analytics.
* **Collaboration**: Teams of 1–4 players.
* **Time Limit**: 90 Minutes.
